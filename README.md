# Business Unit Life Cycle (BULC) — Strategic & Financial Management Framework

A comprehensive framework and data model analyzing the strategic evolution, capital allocation, risk profiles, and corporate finance playbooks for Strategic Business Units (SBUs) across their lifecycle stages.

---

## 📌 Executive Summary

Every business unit—whether a product line, division, subsidiary, or SBU—moves through a predictable sequence of stages: **Introduction**, **Growth**, **Maturity**, and **Decline** (with potential for **Renewal**). 

This repository provides structured data, visual models, and financial playbooks to guide decision-making around:
* **Strategic Positioning:** Aligning unit strategy with market dynamics.
* **Capital Allocation:** Shifting funding sources (Equity, Debt, Internal Cash Flow, Divestitures) as the unit matures.
* **Risk & Investment Balancing:** Evaluating relative investment requirements against operational and execution risks.
* **Portfolio Optimization:** Mapping lifecycle stages to the **BCG Growth-Share Matrix**.

---

## 📊 Core Analytical Models

### 1. Revenue, Profit & Cash Flow Trajectory
Tracks how financial metrics evolve over time:
* **Cash Flow Trough:** Net cash flow troughs before profit turns positive.
* **Profit Peak:** EBIT typically peaks and softens before total revenue declines.

### 2. Investment Need vs. Risk Profile Index (0–100)

| Stage | Investment Need Index | Risk Level Index | Strategic Context |
| :--- | :---: | :---: | :--- |
| **Introduction** | 85 | 90 | High existential risk; unproven market fit. |
| **Growth** | 95 | 70 | Peak capital draw to fund capacity & expansion. |
| **Maturity** | 45 | 30 | Lowest risk profile; strong positive cash generation. |
| **Decline** | 15 | 35 | Capital harvesting; risk ticks up due to obsolescence. |
